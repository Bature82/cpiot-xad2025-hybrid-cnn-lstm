\section*{Data and code availability}
The SWaT dataset is distributed by iTrust, Centre for Research in Cyber
Security, Singapore University of Technology and Design, under a request-based
research agreement, and cannot be redistributed by the authors. The N-BaIoT
dataset is publicly available from the UCI Machine Learning Repository. We
release the corpus-construction script, the alignment diagnostics, the
training and evaluation code, the hyper-parameter grids and the per-run result
tables at \url{https://github.com/Bature82/cpiot-xad2025-hybrid-cnn-lstm}. The
released scripts reproduce CPIoT-XAD2025 from the two source corpora once the
requester has obtained SWaT access from iTrust, and reproduce every table and
figure in this paper from the resulting fused file.
